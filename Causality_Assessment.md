# 3. WHO-UMC CAUSALITY ASSESSMENT SYSTEM

The **WHO-UMC causality assessment system** classifies the relationship between a suspected medicine and an adverse reaction into different categories.

| Causality Category | General Assessment Criteria |
|---|---|
| **Certain** | Reasonable time relationship; cannot be explained by another condition or medicine; response to withdrawal is clinically appropriate; rechallenge, if necessary, is satisfactory. |
| **Probable / Likely** | Reasonable time relationship; unlikely to be explained by another medicine or disease; clinically reasonable response to withdrawal. Rechallenge is not required. |
| **Possible** | Reasonable time relationship, but the reaction could also be explained by another medicine or underlying disease. |
| **Unlikely** | Temporal relationship makes a drug-related cause improbable, although it cannot be completely excluded. |
| **Conditional / Unclassified** | More data are needed for proper assessment, or additional information is being awaited. |
| **Unassessable / Unclassifiable** | Insufficient or contradictory information, and the information cannot be supplemented or verified. |

### WHO-UMC Assessment of This Case

**Causality Category: PROBABLE / LIKELY**

**Reason:**
- Reasonable temporal relationship between Amoxicillin exposure and reaction onset.
- Symptoms improved after discontinuation of Amoxicillin.
- No stronger alternative explanation was identified.
- Rechallenge was not performed.

# 4. NARANJO ADVERSE DRUG REACTION PROBABILITY SCALE

The **Naranjo Scale** is a questionnaire-based method used to estimate the probability that an adverse reaction is related to a suspected drug.

| Naranjo Question | Answer | Score |
|---|---|---:|
| 1. Are there previous conclusive reports of this reaction? | Unknown | 0 |
| 2. Did the adverse event appear after the suspected drug was administered? | Yes | +2 |
| 3. Did the reaction improve when the drug was discontinued? | Yes | +1 |
| 4. Did the reaction reappear on readministration of the drug? | Unknown / Not performed | 0 |
| 5. Are there alternative causes that could have caused the reaction? | No significant alternative cause identified | +2 |
| 6. Did the reaction reappear with placebo? | Unknown | 0 |
| 7. Was the drug detected in blood or other body fluid at toxic levels? | Unknown | 0 |
| 8. Was there a dose-response relationship? | Unknown | 0 |
| 9. Did the patient have a similar reaction to the same or similar drugs previously? | Unknown | 0 |
| 10. Was the adverse event confirmed by objective evidence? | Unknown | 0 |
| **Total Score** | | **5** |

### Naranjo Score Interpretation

| Score | Probability |
|---:|---|
| **≥ 9** | Definite |
| **5–8** | **Probable** |
| **1–4** | Possible |
| **≤ 0** | Doubtful |

### Result

**Naranjo Score: 5**

**Probability: PROBABLE**

The score supports a probable relationship between **Amoxicillin** and the reported adverse reactions.
