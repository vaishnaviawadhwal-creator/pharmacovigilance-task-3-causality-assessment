# TASK 3 – CAUSALITY ASSESSMENT

> **Pharmacovigilance Internship Project – Task 3**

---

## 1. CASE INFORMATION

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| **Patient** | 45-year-old Female |
| **Suspected Drug** | Amoxicillin |
| **Adverse Reaction** | Skin rash, itching, nausea, and dizziness |
| **Onset** | 3 days after starting Amoxicillin |
| **Outcome** | Recovered after discontinuation |
| **Concomitant Medication** | Paracetamol, as needed (PRN) |

---

# 2. CAUSALITY ASSESSMENT

## A. TEMPORAL RELATIONSHIP 

The adverse reactions occurred **3 days after starting Amoxicillin**, indicating a reasonable temporal relationship between drug exposure and the onset of symptoms.

**Assessment:**  Reasonable temporal relationship

---

## B. DECHALLENGE 

The symptoms **improved after discontinuation of Amoxicillin**, indicating a positive dechallenge.

**Assessment:**  Positive dechallenge

---

## C. ALTERNATIVE CAUSES 

No significant alternative cause was identified from the available case information.

Paracetamol was taken only as needed (PRN) and was considered less likely to be responsible for the reported reaction.

**Assessment:**  No significant alternative cause identified

---

## D. RECHALLENGE 

Rechallenge was **not performed**, and no re-exposure to Amoxicillin was documented.

**Assessment:** Not performed

---

# 3. WHO-UMC CAUSALITY CLASSIFICATION

## **PROBABLE / LIKELY**

The case is classified as **Probable/Likely** based on:

- Reasonable temporal relationship with Amoxicillin.
- Positive dechallenge after drug discontinuation.
- No stronger alternative cause identified.
- Rechallenge was not performed.

---

# 4. ASSESSMENT SUMMARY

| Assessment Criteria | Finding |
|---|---|
| **Temporal Relationship** | Reasonable |
| **Dechallenge** | Positive |
| **Alternative Causes** | No significant alternative cause identified |
| **Rechallenge** | Not performed |
| **Final Causality** | **Probable/Likely** |

---

# 5. CONCLUSION

Based on the available case information, **Amoxicillin is considered the suspected drug associated with the reported adverse reactions**.

The reasonable temporal relationship, positive dechallenge, and absence of a stronger alternative explanation support a **Probable/Likely** classification according to the **WHO-UMC causality assessment approach**.
